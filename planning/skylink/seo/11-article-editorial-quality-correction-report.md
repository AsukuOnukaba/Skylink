# Skylink article editorial quality correction report

**Review completed:** 21 November 2026
**Scope:** all 100 published articles
**Editorial standard:** clear professional British English for Nigerian readers, with specialist depth retained where appropriate
**Repository boundary:** this report and all implementation changes are inside the `Skylink` repository.

## Executive finding

The original library was technically complete but editorially weak. Many articles were short outlines containing internal SEO language, defensive writer instructions, compressed metaphors and unexplained technical terms. The public article pipeline now applies an individual plain-English answer to every research ID, expands practical guidance, adds audience-specific examples, explains when professional support is appropriate, refreshes card and CTA copy, records authoritative sources and updates the modification date without changing publication dates, slugs or research IDs.

The automated quality gate checks all 100 public records for minimum depth, authoritative references, the revised date, known internal-language patterns and duplicate long paragraphs. Automated checks support the review but do not replace human editorial judgement.

## Representative before-and-after examples

### Residential

- **Before:** “A realistic timeline explainer: factors, not a stopwatch promise.”
  **After:** “Installation time depends on roof access, mount type, cable distance, permissions, weather and whether power is ready for testing.”

- **Before:** “Home-focused rural planning without inventing coverage maps or NGO case studies.”
  **After:** “Starlink can be useful where fixed broadband is limited, but a rural home still needs service availability, clear sky, reliable power, physical security and a support plan.”

### Equipment

- **Before:** “Cable length as an install factor — generation-correct only.”
  **After:** “A longer Gen 3 cable is useful when the clearest dish position is far from a sensible indoor router location. Measure the route and confirm compatibility before buying.”

- **Before:** “Duty cycle and environment — not status shopping.”
  **After:** “Performance-class hardware becomes relevant when mobility, harsh conditions, wider sky visibility or operational resilience genuinely justify it.”

### Troubleshooting

- **Before:** “Split sky, Wi-Fi and power before blaming the brand.”
  **After:** “Slow service can come from the satellite connection, an obstructed dish, local Wi-Fi, too many active devices or a wider outage. Test close to the router and check the app first.”

- **Before:** “Reboots are often power events wearing a network costume.”
  **After:** “Repeated restarting is commonly caused by unstable electricity, a loose power connection, overheating or damaged equipment.”

### Business

- **Before:** “The dish is the WAN. Premises Wi-Fi is a separate design.”
  **After:** “Starlink supplies the internet connection; a separate network design carries it around the building using suitable wired links and access points.”

- **Before:** “Many addresses, many dishes. Not a GPS product.”
  **After:** “Multi-site businesses need an inventory of each kit, account, address, plan, mount and local contact, plus consistent monitoring and escalation.”

### Local

- **Before:** “Estate-and-district depth for FCT installs, not a second city landing.”
  **After:** “Across Abuja, property type and estate rules matter more than the district name alone. Check access, nearby obstructions, cable distance and dependable power.”

- **Before:** “A hill-and-slope article, not a generic south-east template.”
  **After:** “Enugu's slopes and varying roof heights can change the view of the sky over a short distance, so check at the intended mounting height.”

### Maritime

- **Before:** “Maritime Starlink work is not a residential roof job with salt spray.”
  **After:** “A boat installation must cope with movement, vibration, saltwater exposure, cable sealing, restricted space and vessel power.”

- **Before:** “Survey quality tracks the pack you send.”
  **After:** “Before a maritime survey, provide vessel dimensions, clear photographs, operating routes, proposed mount areas, power details and existing network information.”

### News

- **Before:** “Availability is per address in Starlink's systems. Blogs cannot certify your street.”
  **After:** “The reliable way to check availability is to enter the exact service address in Starlink's official ordering or coverage tools.”

- **Before:** “We will not invent payment methods. Use official account tools.”
  **After:** “Subscription payment methods, prices and foreign-exchange effects can change. Use the options shown in the official Starlink account and confirm the total charge.”

### Forecast

- **Before:** “Plan for mismatch risk. Do not inventory vapourware.”
  **After:** “As Starlink hardware changes, identify each kit precisely and stock mounts, cables and power accessories only for confirmed demand.”

- **Before:** “Backup demand is a trend. Percentages here would be fiction.”
  **After:** “More organisations are likely to consider satellite broadband as a second path because dependence on one provider creates operational risk.”
